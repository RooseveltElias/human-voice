# Structural Patterns: Deep Reference

Before/after examples for each major AI structural pattern.
For use when rewriting AI-generated text or editing for human voice.

---

## 1. Negative Parallelism

AI default:
"It is not a product launch. It is a paradigm shift."
"This is not just about efficiency. It is about transformation."
"It is not what we built. It is how we built it."

Why it is a tell: AI uses this rhetorical move constantly because it sounds
punchy. Human writers use it occasionally and deliberately. When every third
sentence does this, the pattern reveals the machine.

Fix: state the positive directly. Drop the "not X" entirely.
"This changes how we think about growth."
"Speed matters less than what you ship."

---

## 2. Rule of Threes

AI default:
"Innovative, transformative, and groundbreaking."
"Our platform offers speed, reliability, and scalability."
"We focus on people, process, and technology."

Why it is a tell: AI defaults to three items in every list, three adjectives
in every description. The number becomes a fingerprint.

Fix: break the count. Use two, four, one, or none depending on what is real.
"Fast and solid."
"People, process, tools, and the time to use them properly."
"Speed."
Or: rewrite as prose that does not need a list.

---

## 3. False Range

AI default:
"From intimate gatherings to global movements."
"From technical expertise to creative vision."
"From startups to enterprise."
"From the boardroom to the factory floor."

Why it is a tell: the "from X to Y" construction implies a spectrum but usually
names two loosely related things to sound comprehensive. There is no spectrum.

Fix: say what you actually mean.
"Works for both small retailers and large chains."
"Useful whether you are building a proof of concept or scaling to production."
Or: describe the thing directly and skip the framing.

---

## 4. Misconception Framing

AI default:
"Not only is this a financial tool, it is a strategic asset."
"It is not merely a platform; it is an ecosystem."
"While many see this as a cost centre, it is in fact a growth driver."

Why it is a tell: AI writes as though the reader arrived holding a wrong belief
that needs correcting. The "wrong belief" is invented to create contrast.

Fix: state the claim directly.
"This is a strategic asset, not overhead."
"The platform is the ecosystem."

---

## 5. Unearned Importance

AI default (from Wikipedia's actual examples):
"This etymology highlights the enduring legacy of the community's resistance
and the transformative power of unity in shaping its identity."
(Written about a note on how a village name was hispanized.)

"This discovery underscores the remarkable complexity of marine ecosystems
and the critical importance of ongoing conservation efforts."
(Written about noting that a fish species exists in the Pacific.)

Why it is a tell: AI appends significance statements reflexively to facts that
need none. It is the written equivalent of clearing your throat.

Fix: end on the fact. Let it stand.
"The name was hispanized to Bacnotan during Spanish colonial rule."

---

## 6. Compulsive Summary

AI default:
"In conclusion, the evidence suggests that early intervention is key.
Overall, the findings highlight the importance of proactive measures.
In summary, this approach offers the most robust solution."

Why it is a tell: humans summarize long documents. AI summarizes everything,
including three-sentence responses.

Fix: end on the last real point. No summary. The reader just read it.

---

## 7. Participial Phrase Overuse

AI default:
"The system processes the request, generating a detailed response."
"The team reviewed the data, identifying three key opportunities."
"The report covers market dynamics, highlighting the key trends."

Why it is a tell: research documents AI using these constructions at two to
five times the rate of human writers. Multiple per paragraph is a hard tell.

Fix: split or restructure.
"The system processes the request. It generates a detailed response."
"The team reviewed the data and found three key opportunities."
Or restructure: "Three opportunities emerged from the data review."

---

## 8. Compulsive Balance

AI default:
"While there are certainly benefits to this approach, it is also important
to consider the potential drawbacks."
"On one hand, the technology offers significant advantages. On the other
hand, there are legitimate concerns about implementation."
"This can be a powerful tool, though it is worth noting the limitations."

Why it is a tell: AI almost never commits to an unhedged position because
its training penalises confident claims that might be wrong. The result is
writing that sounds perpetually diplomatic.

Fix: take a position. Name it. Earn the nuance by being specific.
"This approach has one real problem: the implementation cost."
"The technology works. The concerns about it are mostly overstated."

## 9. Template Architecture

AI default structure:
Paragraph 1: broad statement about the topic, context, thesis sentence.
Paragraph 2: topic sentence, supporting point, example, mini-conclusion.
Paragraph 3: topic sentence, supporting point, example, mini-conclusion.
Paragraph 4: topic sentence, supporting point, example, mini-conclusion.
Paragraph 5: "In conclusion," plus restatement of thesis plus call to action.

Why it is a tell: this is the five-paragraph essay structure. AI defaults to
it because it saturates training data through student essays, how-to articles,
and corporate copy. The architecture itself signals machine origin.

Fix: break the mould at the opening.
Start with the most interesting fact, not context-setting.
Start with a question, a fragment, a contradiction.
Put the conclusion first, then support it.
Let paragraphs vary in length based on what they need, not a template.

## 10. Formatting as Substitute for Writing

AI default:
**Key Benefits:**
- **Speed:** The system processes requests rapidly
- **Reliability:** Built on enterprise-grade infrastructure
- **Scalability:** Grows with your business

Why it is a tell: AI reaches for structure when it should write prose. The
bolded term plus colon plus definition format is the most recognisable version.
Emojis in headers are a related tell.

Fix: write the sentence.
"The system is fast, built on solid infrastructure, and scales without
requiring re-architecture."

Use formatting when structure genuinely helps comprehension: complex data,
step-by-step instructions, comparison tables. Not as a substitute for prose.

## The Burstiness Fix in Practice

AI output (uniform, medium-length sentences):
"The partnership offers significant advantages for both parties. It enables
access to new markets while reducing operational costs. The agreement includes
revenue sharing terms that benefit each stakeholder. Implementation will begin
in the third quarter."

Human output (bursty, varies deliberately):
"The partnership works.

We get access to Unified Payments' merchant network, 40,000 terminals across
Nigeria, without building our own distribution from scratch. They get a
technology layer their existing contracts do not cover. Clean exchange.

Implementation starts Q3."

The short sentences land. The long ones carry weight. The rhythm is a choice.
