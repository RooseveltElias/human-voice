## Model-Specific Fingerprints

These change as companies adjust training. Check live_patterns.md for updates.

**ChatGPT / GPT series:**
"delve" and "delve into" (most documented single tell)
"Furthermore," and "Moreover," to open paragraphs
Triple-adjective stacks
Essay structure: intro, three body paragraphs, conclusion
"I hope this helps" / "Feel free to ask" as sign-offs

**Claude:**
Em dash overuse as connector (most documented Claude tell)
Compulsive balance: presenting both sides even unprompted
"That said," as a pivot crutch
Longer and more complex sentences than GPT
Measured, thoughtful tone that can read as hedged

**Gemini:**
Strong preference for numbered lists and bullet formats
Declarative sentence structures throughout
"Here are X things to know about..."

## Substitution Approach

The goal is not a fancier synonym. It is the specific, concrete, accurate word
for the thing being described.

"landscape" -- the Nigerian payments market, the UK SME sector, the competitive field
"tapestry" -- list the actual things, do not wrap them in a metaphor
"delve into" -- look at, examine, pull apart, get into
"testament to" -- proof of, shows that, demonstrates, the clearest evidence
"foster collaboration" -- get people working together, build a culture where X
"navigate challenges" -- deal with, handle, work through, fight through
"robust solution" -- describe what actually makes it strong
"seamlessly" -- almost always just delete it
"innovative" -- describe the actual innovation
"transformative" -- describe what actually changes and how
